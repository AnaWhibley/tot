We believe in full transparency. Here's exactly what you'll pay:

- **Annual management fee:** 0.25% of assets under management, charged monthly
- **Transaction fee:** None — we absorb trading costs
- **Withdrawal fee:** None for standard withdrawals (T+2); expedited same-day withdrawals cost £1.50

There are no hidden fees. The total cost is shown on your dashboard in real time.
