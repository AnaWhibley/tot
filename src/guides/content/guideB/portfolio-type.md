We offer three managed portfolio strategies, each with a different balance of asset classes:

- **Conservative** — 80% bonds, 20% equities. Minimal volatility, steady returns.
- **Balanced** — 50% equities, 50% bonds. Moderate risk and growth.
- **Growth** — 80% equities, 20% bonds. Higher long-term potential with increased short-term swings.

Not sure which to pick? Our risk questionnaire will recommend one based on your answers.
