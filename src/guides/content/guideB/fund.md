Add the initial amount you'd like to invest. You can top up at any time, or set up a recurring monthly contribution.

- **Minimum deposit:** £100
- **Instant top-up** from any linked bank account
- **Standing order** — set a monthly date and amount for automatic contributions

Your funds are held in a segregated client account, separate from Meridian's own funds, and covered up to £85,000 by FSCS protection.
