title: Choose your investment start date
---

Select when you'd like your first investment to be executed. Your money will be allocated to your chosen portfolio on that date.

**Accepted date formats:**

- `DD/MM/YYYY` — e.g. ==01/06/2025==
- `MM-DD-YYYY` — e.g. ==06-01-2025==
- `YYYY-MM-DD` — e.g. ==2025-06-01==

The start date must be at least **2 business days** from today to allow for settlement. Markets closed on weekends and public holidays will roll to the next available day.
