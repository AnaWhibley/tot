---

Defining a goal helps us tailor projections and send you meaningful progress updates.

- **Goal name** — e.g. "House deposit", "Retirement", "Emergency fund"
- **Target amount** — how much you want to accumulate
- **Target date** — when you'd like to reach it

You can set multiple goals and link each one to a separate portfolio.
